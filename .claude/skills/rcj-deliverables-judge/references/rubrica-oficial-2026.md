## Reparto total — 56 pts (+2 bonus)

| Componente | Pts máx |
|---|---:|
| Individual Gameplay Tournament | 30 |
| Technical Documentation Paper (TDP) Form | 7 (+2 bonus) |
| Poster Design & Presentation | 5 |
| Group Team Interview | 5 |
| Documentation and Community Contribution | 5 |
| Sportsmanship | 3 |
| Short Form Video TDP | 1 |

**Bonus (vía TDP form):** +1 open-source CAD/PCB/schematics · +1 open-source software.
**Gameplay:** 1º=30, 2º=27, 3º=25, 4º+ baja de a 1 (mín 0). No se "juzga" — es tabla.

**Escala general** (salvo Sportsmanship y Video): Developing=0 · Satisfactory=1 ·
Proficient=3 · Excellent=5. **Aritmética TDP:** 4 criterios × {0,1,3,5} = 20 puntos
internos que el form normaliza a 7 (ej.: 14/20 ≈ 5/7). Poster/Interview/Doc&Community:
promedio de sus criterios escalado al máximo del componente.

## 1. TDP Form (7 pts + 2 bonus) — 4 criterios

### Electrical
- **Developing (0):** "Little to no original pictures, video, CAD renderings, schematics, sketches, or work."
- **Satisfactory (1):** "Documentation provides basic details of electrical design. Includes some original visual artifacts."
- **Proficient (3):** "Documentation provides sufficient detail for a technical reader to replicate the design process."
- **Excellent (5):** "Meets all Proficient criteria AND evaluates use of resources" + "data-driven reasoning."

### Mechanical
- **Developing:** "Documentation lacks a clear design process or visuals. Contains minimal mechanical diagrams."
- **Satisfactory:** "Includes basic steps of mechanical construction. Shows some original work."
- **Proficient:** "Clearly describes mechanical strategy and design iterations. Visuals support the narrative."
- **Excellent:** "Meets all Proficient criteria AND explains design trade-offs and constraints."

### Software
- **Developing:** "Software documentation is missing or unclear. Code snippets are unoriginal, unexplained, or lack context."
- **Satisfactory:** "Some code is explained with comments or screenshots. Includes a basic overview of programming languages."
- **Proficient:** "Provides meaningful insight into code structure and function. Describes algorithmic logic."
- **Excellent:** "Meets all Proficient criteria AND includes version control usage, flowcharts, or pseudocode."

### Presentation
- **Developing:** "Disorganized or incomplete documentation. Poor formatting and navigation."
- **Satisfactory:** "Document is readable and has a basic structure. Sections are labeled."
- **Proficient:** "Well-organized and easy to navigate. Sections clearly labeled and include context."
- **Excellent:** "Meets all Proficient criteria AND includes a clear narrative of the team's journey."

## 2. Poster Design & Presentation (5 pts) — 6 criterios

Requisito duro de las reglas: el póster **no supera A1 (60×84 cm)** y debe
"summarize design documents and present the robot's capabilities in an engaging
format". Idioma de facto: inglés (competencia internacional).

### Abstract
- **Developing:** "Abstract is missing, unclear or wholly incomplete."
- **Satisfactory:** "Abstract is somewhat incomplete OR repeats detailed information already in the poster."
- **Proficient:** "Abstract clearly summarizes each critical component AND uses appropriate scientific language."
- **Excellent:** Proficient + "a clear intent to share actionable knowledge."

### Method, Production & Design
- **Developing:** "Very little to no information about production."
- **Satisfactory:** "Some information about production OR complete information but descriptions unclear."
- **Proficient:** "Complete information about production AND the information is clear and concise."
- **Excellent:** Proficient + "a clear intent to share all actionable knowledge."

### Data, Results & Discussion
- **Developing:** "No data is displayed OR data analysis not relevant to project development."
- **Satisfactory:** "Minor data from testing displayed OR significant data but no major modifications mentioned."
- **Proficient:** "Significant data displayed AND major modifications made AND results displayed clearly."
- **Excellent:** "Demonstrates clear understanding of link between testing, evaluation and modification."

### Photos & Graphics
- **Developing:** "Several photos of poor quality OR not relevant to poster section."
- **Satisfactory:** "Photos relevant but some not labeled or cited."
- **Proficient:** "Photos excellent quality AND appropriately labeled and cited."
- **Excellent:** Proficient + "amount of images appropriate to content presented."

### Layout & Design
- **Developing:** "Poster does not follow logical layout OR contains many spelling/grammatical errors."
- **Satisfactory:** "Poster follows somewhat logical layout OR contains a few spelling/grammatical errors."
- **Proficient:** "Clear and logical layout. Information easy to access. Spelling and grammar error free."
- **Excellent:** Proficient + "original design that effectively highlights team's creativity."

### Presentation (en vivo)
- **Developing:** "Team not present during poster session."
- **Satisfactory:** "Team absent for significant portion OR unable to answer questions adequately."
- **Proficient:** "Team present but regularly absent OR did not actively engage."
- **Excellent:** "Team present entire session AND actively engaged AND answered questions."

## 3. Group Team Interview (5 pts) — 3 criterios

### Teamwork & Communication
- **Developing:** "Team disorganized. One or more members disengaged or dominating. Poor communication."
- **Satisfactory:** "Team showed some coordination, but communication inconsistent or relied on one person."
- **Proficient:** "Team worked together with clear roles. All members contributed meaningfully."
- **Excellent:** "Team showed seamless collaboration. Members supported each other effectively."

### Technical Understanding
- **Developing:** "Team struggled to explain design. Robot could not complete task."
- **Satisfactory:** "Team completed task but with unclear or limited understanding."
- **Proficient:** "Team demonstrated solid understanding of design. Task completed with logical reasoning."
- **Excellent:** "Team showed strong technical fluency and problem-solving. Clear, creative explanation."

### Task Execution & Problem Solving
- **Developing:** "Task attempted with basic or partial functionality. Errors not clearly diagnosed."
- **Satisfactory:** "Task completed or close to completion. Team adapted to challenges."
- **Proficient:** "Task completed efficiently with innovative approaches. Clear strategy."
- **Excellent:** "Task completed efficiently with innovative approaches. Confident problem-solving."

---

## 4. Documentation & Community Contribution (5 pts) — 3 criterios

### TDP
- **Developing:** "TDP not filled, not nearly complete or not filled with meaningful information."
- **Satisfactory:** "TDP filled with all required information. Little care taken to present clearly."
- **Proficient:** "TDP filled with all available information and presented helpfully."
- **Excellent:** "TDP filled, presented well, supplemented with sources and links."

### Poster, Presentation & Interview
- **Developing:** "Poster not created, session not attended or information not shared."
- **Satisfactory:** "Poster created but lacked information. Session attended. Information shared on request."
- **Proficient:** "Informative poster created. Questions answered well. Design highlights shown."
- **Excellent:** "Poster detailed and well-presented. Design shown in way helping others replicate."

### Open Source & Published Materials
- **Developing:** "Nothing shared publicly."
- **Satisfactory:** "Nothing except poster and TDP shared publicly."
- **Proficient:** "At least one or two of testing data, design files, code, or explanations shared publicly."
- **Excellent:** "Everything needed for others to learn published. Contact details available."

## 5. Sportsmanship (3 pts) — escala propia 0/1/2/3

- **Developing (0):** "Disrespectful behavior continued after warnings. Missed over half of matches."
- **Satisfactory (1):** "Team occasionally uncooperative or late. Missed some matches. Behavior improved after reminders."
- **Proficient (2):** "Team consistently respectful and on time. Participated fully. Contributed to positive environment."
- **Excellent (3):** Proficient + "Team actively supported others and set outstanding example."

## 6. Short Form Video TDP (1 pt) — escala 0/1

- **Developing (0):** "Explanation not easy to follow. Competitors cannot understand feature implementation."
- **Satisfactory (1):** "Video easy to follow. Fellow competitor understands how feature was done and why impactful."

Las reglas piden videos que cubran: "robotic demonstration", "design process",
"presentation", "innovation & sustainability". Techo = 1 pt: el criterio es
"¿un competidor PAR entiende cómo se hizo la feature y por qué importa?" — no
producción cinematográfica.

## Requisitos de entrega que el juez verifica (reglas 2026)

- **BOM estandarizada** con: componente/descripción, proveedor/fuente, estado
  (nuevo/reusado), kit vs custom, precios.
- **Open source:** lo entregado "will be shared on GitHub repositories"; hay que
  "credit creators of external work" y cumplir licencias. "Teams should
  prioritize learning and not use complete solutions from others."
- **Mentores:** "must not interact in an imposing manner with teams, robots,
  judges, or the judging process."
- **Students-do-the-work:** el soporte externo es parte del aprendizaje, pero
  "none of the support they receive does the work of competing for the team".
  La vara de las reglas: "ability to explain not only what their robots'
  components do but also how they do it."

## Misión educativa (lente general RoboCup/RCJ)

Del preámbulo 2026: los participantes deben "demonstrate skill in programming,
robotics, electronics and mechatronics" y contribuir "to the advancement of the
community"; "all are expected to compete, learn, have fun, and grow".
Consecuencia para el juez: los descriptores Excellent premian sistemáticamente
**compartir conocimiento accionable** y el **vínculo testing→evaluación→
modificación** — proceso educativo documentado por sobre producto pulido.
Honestidad técnica (declarar qué NO está validado) puntúa mejor que oversell:
un claim inflado que el juez detecta contamina Technical Understanding y la
credibilidad de TODOS los entregables.

## Premios anexos (contexto)

- **Community Award:** votado por los equipos (Documentation / Team Spirit /
  Most Helpful); 1º=3, 2º=2, 3º=1; vale +1 solo como desempate.
- **Certificados:** Outstanding Design · Outstanding Innovation · Outstanding
  Documentation · Exemplary Team · podio Gameplay.

## Qué sondea un juez de Open (visión) en mundiales

Preguntas legítimas de sonda (para mock-interview o para anticipar la sesión de
póster), derivadas de Technical Understanding + students-do-the-work:

1. "Walk me through what happens between the camera seeing the ball and the
   wheels moving." (cadena completa, por integrante según rol)
2. "How do you calibrate vision for THIS venue's lighting? How long does it take?"
3. "Show me data from a test that made you change the design." (el vínculo
   testing→modificación, con el dato real)
4. "What would happen if X died mid-match?" (modos de falla: cámara, gyro, comm)
5. "Why 3 omni wheels and not 4? What did that cost you?" (trade-offs)
6. "Which part did YOU personally build/write? What didn't work first?"
7. Si declaran uso de IA: "What did the AI get wrong that you had to fix?"
   (la respuesta separa autoría real de copy-paste)
